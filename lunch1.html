rice and peas

Takes around half an hour Needs 10 different ingredients Level: Easy

rice and pease is one of the easiest lunch there is. it is very light to eat on an empty stomach and, genuinely the most enjoyable and fullfilling

These are the ingredients:

  • Peas 1/2kg
  • 2 cups of rice
  • 3 big spoons of butter
  • 1 big spoon of salt
  • 1 small spoon of pepper
  • 1/2 kg boiled lamb meat
  • 1/4 cow meat
  • almond 100gm
  • pine 100gm
  • rapeseed oil
  1. Wash and soak the rice with cold water so it's clean and put it on the side!
  2. Boil the lamb meat for at least 30 minutes. And we fry the cow meat so the water and blood is gone and add 1 half small spoon of pepper and salt. And fry the almond and pine with a little bit of rapeseed oil.
  3. Put the butter in the saucepan and add the peas to it and add the pepper and salt we mentioned above for around 10 minutes. Add 3 cups of hot water and once it's boiled we add the rice.
  4. And now when the water boils again, you throw the water and close the pan and lower the temperature.
  5. After 10 minutes you mix them. You add the lamb meat, mix it a little bit and then pour it on a big bowl. Add the cow meat on top and the nuts. And on the side you can add some turkish yoghurt.
