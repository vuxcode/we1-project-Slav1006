spaghetti and bechamel

Takes around half an hour Needs 10 different ingredients Level: Easy

spaghetti and bechamel is one of the best lunch there is. it is very light to eat on an empty stomach and, genuinely the most enjoyable and fullfilling

Follow the following steps:

  • Spaghetti 300g
  • cow meat 200g
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 and a half small spoon of salt
  • half a small spoon of pepper
  • 1l milk
  • 4 big spoons of flour
  • 100g of butter
  • 2 big spoons of tomato puree
  • 200g of cheese
  1. We start with putting out 2 pans, one for boiling the pasta and one for frying the meat. To the pasta we add 1/4 small spoon salt. Cut the onion and then add it to the meat and extra 50g butter.
  2. And we add the tomato pure to the cow meat. And 1 cup of hot water. When the pasta is done, should take around 5 minutes, we add it to the meat and mix it really good!
  3. Bring a tray and put everything in it, pasta and the meat etc. Leave it on the side meanwhile you do the beshamel.
  4. To make the bechamel we fry the butter in a pan, and add the flour and mix a little bit so it gives a golden colour on a low temperature. Add 1 small spoon of salt and add the milk while continously mixing it so it becomes soft.
  5. Now we pour the bechamel on top of the tray so the pasta is not visible. And now we sprinkle the cheese on top of the tray and put it in the oven on max for around 10 minutes so it becomes a bit gold.
