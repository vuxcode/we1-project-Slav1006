Falafel

Takes around half an hour Needs 7 different ingredients Level: Easy

Home made falafel are one of the most delicious appetizers there is. They're perhaps not the lightest food to eat on an empty stomach but, genuinely the most enjoyable and fullfilling

Follow the following steps:

  • Hummus chickpeas 1kg
  • 1 onion
  • Cumin 1 big spoon
  • Coriander seeds 2 big spoons
  • dried pepper 1 big spoon
  • salt 1 big spoon
  • Baking soda
  1. Soak the hummus chickpeas 24 hours beforehand with cold water!
  2. After the 24 hours hours we take the hummus out of the water and then grind it with the onion in a mixer.
  3. After you have mixed them, you add the spices.
  4. After you blended everything, you need to have a special kind of spoon, for the form of the falafel.
  5. In the meantime you put the oil on the stove till it's warm. Afer the oil is warm, you dip the falafel in sesame before putting them into the oil. Then you dip the falafel in it until they are crispy and giving a gold colour.
