Nescafe biscuits

Takes around 20 minutes Needs 5 different ingredients Level: Easy

Nescafe biscuits is my favourite dessert there is. it is very light to eat on an empty stomach and, genuinely the most enjoyable and fullfilling

These are the ingredients:

  • a bag tea biscuits
  • Chantilly cream 24g
  • mascarpone
  • 3/4 cold milk
  • 2 big spoons of nescafe
  1. First we need a small bowl, put 1 cup of hot water and 1 big spoon of nescafe. We need a rectangles shaped tray.
  2. Take out another big bown and pour the cold milk in it, and 1 big spoon of nescafe and whisl it. And then you add the mascarpone and mix it really good and now the cream is ready.
  3. Now we take out the biscuits, and one by one you dip it really quick into the small bowl and then into the tray. Make sure to put the biscuits side by side.
  4. After putting the first layer with biscuits, now you add the cream we made. And now once again you continue with the biscuits.
  5. And now in the last step you add the rest of the cream. Now when it is done you can sprinkle a little bit of nescafe to the top for the style
